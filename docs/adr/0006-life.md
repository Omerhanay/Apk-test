# ADR 0006: Life tab (people, things, timeline)

Status: accepted (2026-09-24)

## Decision
- **People and things are `entities`** added by the user (`source = user_stated`).
  People carry a relation (spouse, child, parent, …); things a type (vehicle,
  home, subscription, device, pet, other). Documents keep creating their own
  entities; those stay under Documents and are not listed as things.
- **Dates live in `entity_dates`.** A date can repeat every year
  (`FREQ=YEARLY`) and carry `remind_days_before` (schema v4). A birthday whose
  year is unknown is stored in year 2000 (a leap year, so 29 February fits) and
  no age is shown; in common years 29 February falls on 28 February.
- **Reminders are ordinary planner reminders** at 09:00, N days before, targeted
  at the date row. A one-off date whose reminder day has passed gets none. For
  a yearly date, the next year's reminder is armed; after unlock,
  `ensureUpcomingReminders` re-arms any yearly date without a future reminder,
  so birthdays keep coming without a background job.
- **Related items are found by Turkish-aware text match** of the name against
  current memories, open tasks and documents. Nothing is inferred or linked
  automatically.
- **The timeline is computed, not stored**: events, due and completed tasks,
  added documents, remembered facts and upcoming entity dates, from 90 days
  back to 60 days ahead, recomputed when any table changes.
- **Delete is complete**: the entity, its dates, their reminders and its
  relationships.
